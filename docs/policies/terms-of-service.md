**Status: draft, not yet published anywhere.** Copy-ready text for

---

## Using this site

This site is a public reading room, a reservation desk, and a membership
office, in that order. Reading published content, sending a message, and
looking at the calendar require nothing from you. Applying for
membership, reserving a table, or registering for a programme means
giving PAZ the information described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy)
so those things can actually happen.

**Fair use.** The public site (search, articles, the Record) is open to
everyone, including automated readers, within reason — public forms
(Pigeon Post, contact, membership applications) are rate-limited to stop
abuse; the archive itself is not.

## Membership

- A membership term runs one year from the date it starts, at the fee
  for the tier you applied for.
- Applications are reviewed by staff and may be accepted or declined; a
  decision is not a guarantee.
- Payment can currently be made by cash or bank transfer, recorded by
  staff; online payment via eSewa or Khalti may be offered once available
  (see the Privacy Policy for what that involves).
- A term that lapses without renewal simply ends — there is no automatic
  recurring charge of any kind.
- Membership numbers are permanent once issued and are not reused, even
  if a membership lapses and is later renewed.

## Reservations (the Hearth)

Every reservation is confirmed by a person, not instantly — expect a
reply, not an automatic confirmation. A submitted request is not a
guarantee of a table until you hear back.

## Content you send PAZ

**Pigeon Post and the contact form.** Anything you submit may be read,
replied to, and — for Pigeon Post specifically — considered for editing
and publication under PAZ's own editorial process. Submitting something
does not guarantee it will be published, and PAZ may edit accepted
submissions before publication.

## PAZ's published content

Everything PAZ publishes — Papers, Brief, Dispatch, Pigeon Post, the
Annual, and the Record — is PAZ's own institutional work (or published
with the contributor's permission). You're welcome to read, quote, and
link to it; reproducing it in full elsewhere, or using it commercially,
needs PAZ's permission first. Reach out through the Contact page to ask.

## Payments

If online payment is enabled: transactions are processed entirely by
eSewa or Khalti, PAZ's own payment partners — their terms govern the
payment itself. PAZ records only that a payment happened and its
reference number, never your card, bank, or wallet details.

## Changes to these terms

If these terms change in a way that matters, PAZ will post an updated
version here with a visible date.

## Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Nepal.
